# 🎂 AgeWish - Age Calculator & Wish Card Maker

**AgeWish** is a beautifully designed, 100% offline Android app that calculates your exact age, tracks upcoming birthdays, and generates shareable PDF wish cards for birthdays and anniversaries.

## ✨ Features
- 🧮 **Precise Age Calculator:** Get your age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes.
- 📅 **Date Difference Calculator:** Find the exact time between two dates (including working days).
- 🎁 **Wish Card Maker:** Generate beautiful PDF greeting cards for Birthdays, Anniversaries, Weddings, Diwali, Eid, and more.
- 🎨 **Custom Themes:** Choose from multiple colors for your wish cards.
- 📤 **Share & Save:** Export your reports and wish cards as PDFs and share them via WhatsApp, Gmail, or Google Drive.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode:** Full Material 3 support with an elegant dark theme.
- 🔒 **100% Offline:** No servers, no tracking, no data collection.

## 🚀 Installation
You can install AgeWish directly on your Android device.

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/CartonIQ/AgeWish/releases)** page.
2. Download the latest `AgeWish.apk` file.
3. Open the file on your phone and tap **Install** (you may need to allow "Install from unknown sources").

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Material 3, XML Layouts, ViewBinding
- **Architecture:** MVVM, Navigation Component
- **Local Database:** Room DB
- **PDF Generation:** Android Native `PdfDocument` API
- **Monetization:** Google AdMob
